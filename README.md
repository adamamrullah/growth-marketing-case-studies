## Methodology and disclosure

Figures refer to specific historical contexts; they are not comparisons with current performance and cannot establish causation on their own. Baselines, sample sizes and channel contribution should be added where disclosure is authorized. No private customer lists or screenshots from restricted accounts are included.

## What good evidence would look like

- Before/after time windows and original metric definitions
- Measurable strategy changes and experiment timelines
- Redacted analytics screen captures with permission
- Attribution caveats and what did **not** work
- Supporting references, endorsements or case-study sign-off where possible
