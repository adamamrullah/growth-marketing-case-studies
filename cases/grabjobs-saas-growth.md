# GrabJobs — SaaS Growth in Indonesia

**Role:** Growth Manager (historical role)  
**Focus:** B2B SaaS demand generation, activation, partnerships and growth experimentation.

## Business challenge

Grow employer adoption and recurring revenue for an online hiring platform in a developing market.

## Approach

The work spanned customer acquisition, market positioning, partner outreach, experimentation, and optimization of acquisition journeys. The specific impact of each initiative has not been isolated in this public case study.

## Historical results reported

- SaaS subscribers increased by approximately **450% over six months** in Indonesia.
- Indonesia MRR increased from approximately **$211 to more than $11,000 within a year**.

These periods are reported from career history; they may not align exactly. This case study does not claim that any single channel caused the gains.

## What I would measure in a reproducible retrospective

Qualified employer leads → trial / first action → paid conversion → MRR; acquisition source, conversion cohort, CAC, payback period and churn. Before-and-after raw data is not published here.

## Lessons and caveats

For SaaS growth, acquisition volume alone is not enough: conversion and retention matter. Revenue attribution requires controlled time windows and consideration of product, sales and market factors.
