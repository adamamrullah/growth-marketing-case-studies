# UK Property — Meta Ads Lead Generation

**Role:** Freelance / consulting engagement  
**Channel:** Meta Ads  
**Goal:** Generate relevant property enquiries efficiently.

## Problem

Create a steady flow of leads with clear cost control while maintaining useful qualification criteria.

## Reported outcome

A previously reported campaign-level **cost per lead of approximately £16**, compared in project discussions to informal market expectations around £50–£60. The comparator is not a verified benchmark and should not be presented as a statistically representative market average.

## Approach areas

- Audience and offer alignment
- Messaging and creative experimentation
- Lead capture friction and qualification
- Funnel performance and CPL review

The specific combinations and winning variants are not documented in the accessible public record, so this is deliberately not portrayed as a fully reconstructed campaign.

## What remains to validate

Campaign dates, spend, leads, definition of lead quality, CRM-to-sale rate and published client permission. Avoid extrapolating to other property markets without tests.
