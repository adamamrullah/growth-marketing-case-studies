# GrabJobs — SEO and Organic Acquisition

**Focus:** Search demand, scalable content, partnerships and authority building.

## Business challenge

Capture job-seeker and employer search demand across multiple market segments without relying entirely on paid acquisition.

## Reported approach

SEO work included content strategy, topic clusters, search opportunity prioritization, and link/partnership development. The exact implementation and relative contribution of each tactic are not independently verified here.

## Historical outcome

Previously reported search performance at a scale of **more than 1 million monthly organic clicks**. This is an approximate historical figure, not a current traffic claim or a claim that all traffic was generated by one initiative.

## Framework

1. Segment intent by job-seeker and employer needs.
2. Map keyword opportunities to content and landing-page types.
3. Improve relevance, internal navigation and on-page SEO.
4. Expand topic coverage and track indexation and page quality.
5. Review GSC clicks, impressions, CTR and downstream business conversion.

## Caveats

Organic clicks are not revenue and attribution is multi-factor. A verifiable case study should add time period, market scope, GSC screenshots cleared for publication, and conversion impact if available.
